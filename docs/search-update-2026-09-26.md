## Applied Scope

The main list requires a biomedical or clinical evaluated task, a modeled system state, a state-transition mechanism, an operational forecast or rollout from that state, and evaluated implementation. For this update, a learned or hybrid transition is required, consistent with the 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-20 rechecks. A model name, temporal encoder, one-step representation probe, static treatment score, or digital visualization is insufficient. Autonomous dynamics need no intervention input; a treatment or control claim must specify how the action changes the next state. Existing older mechanistic entries were not reclassified here.

## Four Papers Added to the Main List

**129 -> 133 unique main-list papers.** The radiotherapy paper below extends an already listed method, so these are four distinct papers but not four independent model families.

| Paper | Primary evidence and scope decision |
|---|---|
| [HealthFlux: *A world model simulates the latent dynamics of human health*](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.19.26363460v1.full), medRxiv, posted 2026-09-21 | **Include.** The [Methods](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.19.26363460v1.full) specify a neural-ODE transition for the deterministic patient-health state, a transition prior and observation-conditioned recurrent jumps. Next-event identity and timing train the dynamics. The information-blackout experiment advances the latent state without future observations for one to five years and evaluates disease incidence in the following five-year window. This is a clinical forecast/simulation task, not an evaluated treatment simulator. The paper reports 195 diseases plus death in its primary five-year comparison; no public author code was verified. |
| [PHAROS: *Turning single-cell perturbation models into target-directed drug-combination screens*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.08.749477v1.full), bioRxiv, September 2026 | **Include.** PHAROS recursively applies the frozen [STATE](https://github.com/ArcInstitute/state) drug-conditioned cell-state transition model to the predicted output of the previous drug step, evaluates both two-drug orders and searches paths toward a target cellular state. The paper evaluates exact or mechanism-matched combination recovery in two experimental cell-line datasets. Its [author implementation](https://github.com/jbezney61/PHAROS) and [analysis workflows](https://github.com/jbezney61/PHAROS_reproduce) expose the sequential-screening procedure. The patient-derived tumor applications are exploratory and flagged by the authors as outside supported distribution; experimental treatment-order effects and patient benefit are not established. |
| [CellTempo: *Forecasting cell state futures from static snapshots*](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.02.08.704720v2.full), bioRxiv v2, posted 2026-07-31 | **Include as a backfill.** A VQ-VAE converts transcriptomes to discrete cell codes, and a causal transformer autoregressively generates later cell states; [author code](https://github.com/EperLuo/CellTempo) exposes trajectory generation and evaluation. The paper evaluates multi-step lineage forecasts and propagates predicted initial compound responses through later cell-fate trajectories, including an oligodendrocyte-progenitor induction analysis. Its pretraining trajectories were constructed from snapshots using pseudotime, RNA velocity and inferred transition probabilities, rather than direct cell tracking. The drug analysis is in silico and does not validate clinical efficacy or a drug-conditioned transition at every later step. |
| [*A digital-twin framework for forecasting treatment-day imaging with contour uncertainty in adaptive proton radiotherapy*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24725), arXiv, submitted 2026-09-21 | **Include as a distinct extension of the listed [MICCAI workshop paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00831).** The full PDF's Sections 3-5 describe transferring observed planning-to-weekly-CT changes from 88 prior patients through frozen learned registration onto a current patient's planning CT, producing a patient-specific ensemble of later CT and contour states. The extension adds first-QACT refinement and direction-resolved contour uncertainty, evaluated on 10 patients with clinician contours on two later CTs each. The library-only consensus contour did not improve point Dice over the planning contour; the uncertainty ranked directions but was not Gaussian-calibrated. The paper supports anatomical forecasting, not treatment-response or dose-benefit claims. |

### Source and Implementation Limits

- HealthFlux's current [medRxiv full text](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.09.19.26363460v1.full) reports 195 diseases, a five-year primary comparison and no medication-conditioned trial experiment. A same-DOI Europe PMC preprint record returned a different abstract mentioning 1,010 diseases, twenty years and 36 clinical trials. The catalog uses the directly inspected medRxiv version and does not import those inconsistent claims.
- The PHAROS paper's biological validation compares predicted two-step paths with observed combination-treated endpoint states. It does not establish that the experimental combination was administered in the model's selected order. Code and primary paper were inspected; the large pretrained models and experimental datasets were not run locally.
- CellTempo's author repository currently includes a `TODO` for long-term drug-trajectory reproduction, despite supplying general trajectory inference code and a drug-perturbation entry point. The catalog credits the paper's reported evaluation and available code, not fully reproduced drug experiments.
- The radiotherapy PDF was read locally because arXiv did not provide HTML for this version. Its new contribution is uncertainty and refinement of a previously cataloged transition-transfer pipeline; the entry explicitly records the overlap and negative point-forecast result.
