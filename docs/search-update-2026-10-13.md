## Applied Scope

An entry needs a biomedical or healthcare-evaluated task, an explicit state, an implemented **learned or computed** transition, an operational forecast/simulation/planning/control use, and evaluation of that use. The written scope accepts autonomous cellular, molecular, anatomical and physiological dynamics without action conditioning. Treatment and control labels require proposed actions to change predicted subsequent states. A stateful forecast can qualify without establishing a causal treatment effect; a mechanistic simulator can qualify without being a learned world model. We distinguish those cases in the notes rather than treating titles such as “digital twin” as evidence.

## Twelve Papers Added

**141 → 153 unique main-list papers.** Each also appears once in the topic index. The two cardiac papers are related 2D and 3D methods, not independent clinical validations of the same claim.

| Paper | State transition, evaluation and limit |
|---|---|
| [Finsler Flow Matching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11318) | **Include as cell-state dynamics.** A learned continuous stochastic process carries cells through a directed transition graph; ODE/SDE integration reconstructs withheld intermediate pancreatic populations. This is trajectory inference from snapshots, with stronger reported results in 20/50 dimensions than 2D. The interpolation parameter is not measured biological time, and no treatment-response rollout was tested. |
| [Sign-Constrained Intervention Effects for Domain-Generalizable ICU World Models (PhysioWorld)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11090) | **Include as action-conditioned physiological forecasting.** Event/drug/ventilator inputs change a recurrent state transition; the paper evaluates one-step and eight-step free-running vital-sign forecasts across MIMIC and VitalDB cohorts and probes dose responses. Retrospective observational data cannot identify the causal effect of an unobserved intervention. |
| [Personalized Models of Brain Disorders](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10850) | **Include as brain-state forecasting.** Each subject has a transition distribution over EEG-derived latent states. Held-out next-state prediction and epilepsy-associated transition structure were evaluated in 198 subjects. No long free-running disease forecast, intervention policy, or clinical diagnostic performance was established. |
| [MD-LLM-2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10879) | **Include as molecular dynamics.** Autoregressive structure tokens update protein conformations under sequence, temperature and history. Reported tests cover unseen-domain structural trajectories, folding/compaction and transition kinetics against molecular-dynamics references. This is a protein-folding computation, not a drug-efficacy or patient model. |
| [UltraWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09785) | **Include as an ultrasound view-transition model.** A self-distilled, probe-action-conditioned observation model predicts views for imagined local probe motion; simulated closed-loop scanning tests planning. Clinical ultrasound videos provide a training prior and held-out image-consistency tests, but no real-robot closed-loop navigation was demonstrated. |
| [One Frame, Full Heartbeat: 2D cardiac cine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09397) | **Include as anatomical motion simulation.** Phase-conditioned learned displacement fields transform one end-diastolic MRI frame into an unobserved cardiac cycle; volume and ejection-fraction trajectories were evaluated on ACDC and external M&Ms cases. The phase template is pathology-level, not measured patient-specific cardiac timing. |
| [One Frame, Full Heartbeat: 3D/4D cardiac cine](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.09185) | **Include as anatomical motion simulation.** A radial-contraction prior plus learned residual deformation predicts phase-conditioned 3D displacement fields and a 4D volume cycle from one end-diastolic image. The motion prior is not a validated mechanistic heart simulator, and phase is population-derived. |
| [Identifiable Inverse Optimal Transport for Single-Cell State Transitions](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.01.756056v1) | **Include as cell-state transition forecasting.** PERSIST-IOT estimates a continuous-time generator from cell histories and propagates clone/population composition with transition matrices; a separate CellTag experiment tests future composition. Treatment-response associations are descriptive; candidate-treatment effects were not rolled out and validated. |
| [ProCut](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07515) | **Include under the README's computed-dynamics branch.** A differentiable particle-based tissue model advances geometry/topology after cutting commands, with a belief update and closed-loop dissection. Tests include simulation and robot cutting of ex-vivo chicken skin. The transition is primarily mechanistic, and this is neither an in-patient trial nor evidence of clinical benefit. |
| [HyperNSDE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.07383) | **Include as longitudinal patient simulation.** Static covariates condition the drift/diffusion of a neural SDE that generates irregular measurements and observation times; evaluations use colorectal-cancer and Parkinson's cohorts. It does not update a patient's state from an observed trajectory during generation or estimate treatment-action effects. |
| [APOD](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06227) | **Include as discovered pharmacological dynamics.** The agent proposes and fits executable ODEs for dose-dependent tumor and platelet states. In a radioligand cohort, a model fit on 44 patients forecasts later platelet nadirs in an independent 19-patient temporal cohort after first-cycle personalization. Alternative dose schedules are simulated projections, not observed treatment-benefit evidence. |
| [Ultrasound Operator Guidance](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06008) | **Include as nonparametric action-state planning.** A learned ultrasound embedding indexes recorded probe-action transitions; receding-horizon retrieval plans multistep motions. Retrospective navigation and a three-volunteer prospective feasibility study test target-view reaching. The deployed transition operator retrieves observed transitions rather than generating new video frames. |

### Scope Recheck

All twelve have a medically grounded represented state, an explicit transition mechanism, an operational future-state use and an evaluation of that use. Their capabilities differ: the EEG model tests one-step forecasting, the cardiac models simulate phase-indexed motion, the cell and patient models propagate autonomous states, PhysioWorld and APOD condition physiology on inputs, and the ultrasound/surgical systems use action-dependent transitions for planning or control. `ProCut` is expressly a **computed** simulator. `Ultrasound Operator Guidance` uses a **retrieved**, nonparametric transition database. Those distinctions keep the main list aligned with the README's broader scope without calling every paper a generative, learned or clinically validated world model.

| [STAVelo](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.10.02.756177) | **Hold for methods access.** The accessible primary page did not supply sufficient full methods to distinguish temporal state propagation from velocity inference alone. |
