## Applied Scope

For new core entries, require a directly biomedical task, a modeled biological or clinical state, learned or hybrid dynamics that advance that state, an executed forecast or simulation, and evaluated implementation. A perturbation-conditioned endpoint map, internal recurrent encoder, static risk score, or simulator proposal is not enough. Autonomous dynamics need no action input; intervention claims must show how the intervention changes the propagated state. This follows the stricter interpretation in the 2026-09-08 and 2026-09-20 rechecks without retroactively reclassifying older mechanistic entries.

### Metadata Boundaries

- AnnFlux receives `Temporal + Action-conditioned`: the learned drift depends on the perturbing gene or drug object. Its combination tests do not establish sequential treatment planning.
- MEGA-ODE receives `Temporal`, without a separate action-conditioning label. Its released model integrates from a molecular input state; virtual overexpression modifies that state, rather than supplying a treatment action at every transition. `Forecast + Simulate` does not imply a validated optimization policy.
- RegVelo receives `Forecast + Simulate` for ODE evolution and Markov fate prediction under virtual knockouts. The label does not equate fate probabilities with observed cell-by-cell trajectories.
