## Scope Used for the Decisions

The opening README definition emphasizes learned dynamics, whereas the operational inclusion table and contribution guide explicitly permit **learned or computed state transitions**. The September audits also record a narrower learned/hybrid interpretation, while the October audits follow the broader written rule. This inconsistency predates this audit.

For this recheck:

- The current written criteria govern catalog edits: biomedical state, implemented state transition, operational forecast/simulation/planning/control, and evaluation.
- Learning the forward transition, discovering an equation, learning parameters of a fixed equation, and transferring image deformations are different contributions. They are labeled separately rather than all being described as learned world models.
- Historical-treatment prediction, neural-network recurrence, model updates after new observations, or generation of an entire conditional profile does not automatically establish intervention-conditioned world dynamics.
- A model-based counterfactual is a projection under specified assumptions. Covariate sensitivity is not an identified treatment effect. In-silico control is not a clinically validated controller.
- Lack of treatment actions alone does not disqualify autonomous dynamics. Lack of public full text alone is also not a negative finding: the question is what the accessible evidence actually establishes.
- Conditional endpoint or field prediction must establish evaluated state evolution to meet the catalog's dynamics and operational-rollout criteria.

A future **learned/hybrid-only** catalog would keep L as its most direct twin-related fit, and would need an explicit editorial decision about F, M and T across the whole catalog. This audit does not silently impose that scope change. M papers are retained under the present computed-dynamics rule, not certified as learned dynamics.
