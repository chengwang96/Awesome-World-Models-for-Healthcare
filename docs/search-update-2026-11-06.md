The governing scope is the current README and contribution guide: a biomedical or healthcare state, an implemented **learned or computed transition**, an operational forecast/simulation/planning/control use, and evaluation of that use. Autonomous physiology and cellular development can qualify without treatment actions. A treatment or control capability requires actions to affect subsequent system states. Internal neural-network iteration, evidence-record updates, temporal pretraining, or a drug-conditioned endpoint alone does not establish an operational medical world model.

Some September audits used a narrower learned/hybrid-only rule. This pass follows the written scope and the October 13/16 interpretation, identifies computed dynamics explicitly, and does not silently apply that older restriction or retrospectively rewrite those audits. Neither the overall scope nor the controlled taxonomy was changed. Inclusion does not require clinical efficacy, but the entry must describe the evidence actually available.

**160 → 167 unique local main-list papers.** The initial draft added ten papers (170 total); subsequent maintainer review removed three, leaving seven additions. Every retained new entry is mirrored in the relevant topic sections, with identical titles and links. Multiple topic placements do not increase the unique-paper count.

| Paper and source date | State transition, evaluated use, and limitation |
|---|---|
| [SurgTransfer](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.05443v1), November 3 | **Include as action-conditioned surgical simulation.** A multi-step latent-action encoder extracts a prerecorded action sequence from a source video; a diffusion dynamics model applies it to another initial scene. Seven-frame chunks are chained for 14-frame evaluations on SurgToolLoc and PitVis, with video/motion/cycle-consistency metrics and five surgeons assessing 40 generated videos. The transferred actions are not measured robot commands, and the paper explicitly says predefined trajectories prevent truly interactive use. Anatomy, procedure and embodiment must remain similar. [Author code](https://github.com/bhattarailab/Surgical-Vision-World-Model) now contains both SurgTransfer and the older SurgWM, which is a separate existing catalog entry. The author README reports WACV 2027 acceptance; the catalog retains the verified 2026 arXiv source/year pending official proceedings/program confirmation. |
| [PulmoWorld](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.38289v1), October 28 | **Include as recursive multimodal patient dynamics.** A belief state propagates between observations, accepts masked measurement corrections, and advances without factual intermediate inputs. Four-step clinical forecasts are tested on 300 locked MIMIC pneumonia patients and 40 locked MASS patients, with separately trained cohort models; imaging outputs are representation forecasts, not generated scans. Care-conditioned factual forecasting and five known-mechanism MASS-Syn planning environments are distinct experiments. `Plan` refers only to the latter; the reported regret improvement does not establish treatment benefit in real patients. Appendix E promises a future code release, so the entry remains `Paper only`. |
| [Tumor digital twins uncover therapeutic strategies in triple-negative breast cancer](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2026.103119), October 26 | **Include as computed, action-conditioned signaling dynamics.** The [STAR Methods](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666379126005367#4) define a single synchronous successor state from node target functions, iterated to attractors or analyzed for attractor bounds. Patient genomic alterations and drug inhibitions change these transitions. A literature specification informs refinement, followed by response comparison in 31 PARTNER patients and virtual resensitization screens. The reported response accuracy is 74%; subsequent screens focus on the correctly modeled subset. This is an untimed qualitative network, not a tumor-volume trajectory, PK/PD model or dose-scheduling simulator. Candidate therapeutic benefit is untested prospectively. [Public code](https://github.com/jfisher-lab/TNBC_Victori2026) supplies analysis scripts; the executable network specification is described in the paper's supplementary materials. |
| [Digital Twin Workflow for Continuous Glucose Forecasting](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366163v1), posted October 24 | **Include as an autonomous physiological forecast extension.** Methods 2.1.4 and the [PDF](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.10.23.26366163v1.full.pdf), pp. 6–7, explicitly feed predicted glucose back into the window for a 15-step trajectory. A2C corrects only the first forecast step; subsequent predictions use the frozen PatchTST. Retrospective evaluation uses ten septic ICU patients and builds on the already listed [earlier glucose-twin study](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44324-026-00132-2). This is a distinct methodological extension, not evidence from an independent new clinical trial. RL actions adjust forecast residuals, not insulin or another physiological intervention, so the entry receives `Forecast` only. |
| [DuoPIC](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33071v1), October 20 | **Include as hybrid tracer-transport simulation.** Blood flow drives a time-evolving transport PDE with a population-trained neural closure; evaluations cover reconstruction, patient-held-out closure use, hidden temporal blocks, uncertainty and stroke lesion maps on ISLES MRI/CT. The [hidden-frame evaluation code](https://github.com/detinatran/duopic/blob/main/scripts/eval_hidden_frames.py) refits after masking contiguous blocks, using the other frames; it offers cold and warm starts. This is not a strict observation-blackout forecast. Accordingly the final entry is labeled `Simulate` only. No reperfusion-treatment response or longitudinal disease forecast is validated. [Code and protocol](https://github.com/detinatran/duopic); the repository identifies ISBI 2027 as submitted, not accepted. |
| [Saga](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.33054v1), October 19 | **Include as event-driven oncology patient simulation.** Treatment/time-conditioned patient states produce clinical events, waiting times and modality-specific future readouts. Generated events/times are appended and the history is re-encoded, with matched-origin free-rollout comparisons through eight steps. Paired future-image evaluation and temporal/geographical clinical evaluations are additional, separate tests. The headline cohort comprises 461,370 patients, but task-specific eligible populations differ. Conditional output changes do not identify treatment effects, and planning or clinical utility is not tested. No released model/code destination was verified. |
| [CaMeWM](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.26923v1), October 17 | **Include as treatment-to-state simulation and bounded planning.** An action-conditioned response descriptor, deterministic anchor and residual 3D diffusion generate a lesion-local follow-up CT for a supported TACE regimen. Sampled worlds feed a survival utility for treatment ranking. Five-fold evaluation uses 79 eligible paired episodes and two regimens; factual transition scores and cross-fitted, censoring-adjusted AIPW policy-value estimates are separate evidence. The positive estimated RMST contrast depends on observational identification and censoring assumptions, not prospective benefit. The implementation models one post-treatment transition, not a multi-step adaptive treatment policy. No verified public code link was found. |

### Second-Pass Capability Check

The seven retained additions do not all demonstrate the same type of world model. SurgTransfer executes video continuations from transferred latent actions; PulmoWorld and Saga recursively generate patient states; the glucose extension forecasts an autonomous signal; DuoPIC computes time-resolved physiology with a learned closure; the TNBC network advances untimed biological signaling states; CaMeWM computes a single intervention-to-follow-up transition used in regimen ranking. These mechanisms, rather than model names, support the screening decisions.

The metadata deliberately omits `Counterfactual` and `Control` for all seven retained new entries. `Plan` is limited to PulmoWorld's synthetic action selection, CaMeWM's supported-regimen ranking, and the TNBC model's virtual target selection. Computational and observational results are not presented as clinical treatment-effect validation. The glucose and DuoPIC entries were specifically checked to avoid equating forecast correction with physiological control or masked reconstruction with prospective forecasting.

## Two Perspectives and One Benchmark

- [From Prediction to Action: Population Health World Model for Digital Medicine](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.24662v2), initially October 17, revised October 30, develops a population-health research agenda and requirements. Added to **Survey Papers**, not the implemented-model list.
- [Toward Verifiable Medical Imaging World Models](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10278-026-02413-0), *Journal of Imaging Informatics in Medicine*, October 21; [PMID 42907729](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42907729/). This methodological perspective/reporting framework audits nine existing imaging world-model papers and proposes fidelity, stability and reliability criteria. It does not introduce a new implemented medical dynamics model. Added to **Survey Papers**.
- [Evaluating Type-1 Diabetes Digital Twins by Decision Quality](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30577v1), October 22, supplies a [public benchmark implementation](https://github.com/Veeeeeee7/T1D-Digital-Twin-Benchmark). Thirty UVA/Padova virtual patients are simulated under 21 basal-bolus treatments; each fitted twin predicts trajectories for 20 alternatives. The benchmark measures glucose prediction error, treatment ranking and regret, and demonstrates that these criteria need not agree. Added to **Benchmarks** only: the contribution is a comparison protocol, not a separately counted new world-model method. The ground truth is simulated, not a clinical treatment experiment.

The survey section grows from **18 to 20** entries; the benchmark table from **4 to 5**. Neither affects the main-list count.
