## Applied Scope

Each new main-list entry must have a directly biomedical or healthcare-evaluated task, a represented biological/clinical/procedure/population state, an implemented learned or hybrid state transition, an operational forecast/simulation/planning/control use, and evaluated evidence. The written scope permits autonomous forecasting without action conditioning. We label action-dependent treatment or policy effects only where candidate actions enter subsequent-state predictions. A method can therefore qualify as a *forecast model* without establishing counterfactual control.

## Five Papers Added

**136 → 141 unique main-list papers.** Four are dated 2026-09-28 to 2026-10-01, and one is a 2026-08-20 backfill. The head-and-neck paper is a new evaluation of an already cataloged model family, not an independent dynamics architecture.

| Paper | State, transition, operational test, and scope boundary |
|---|---|
| [A Digital Twin for Tracking and Forecasting Glycemia with Septic Patients in ICUs](https://doi.org/10.1038/s44324-026-00132-2), *npj Metabolic Health and Disease*, published 2026-10-01 | **Include as physiology forecasting.** Its [methods and results](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44324-026-00132-2) describe a PatchTST model taking 30 minutes of continuous glucose values and predicting 15- and 30-minute future glucose trajectories. The pretrained template came from **one** septic patient; personalization and rolling error monitoring were evaluated retrospectively on ten further patients. This is a one-variable, direct-horizon forecast, not an insulin/nutrition-conditioned glucose simulator. Treatment optimization, prospective bedside use, and improved patient outcomes were not evaluated. |
| [An Uncertainty-Guided Digital Twin Framework for Online Adaptive Proton Therapy in Head and Neck Cancer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39010), arXiv, submitted 2026-09-30 | **Include as a forecast extension.** The full [paper and methods](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.39010) transfer observed longitudinal deformations from prior patients through pretrained CT registration onto a planning CT, producing future anatomy/contour ensembles and direction-specific margins; each test patient's own longitudinal sessions were excluded from its reference library. It adds plan generation on forecast CTs, selection against the acquired treatment-day QACT, and reoptimization; plan quality was evaluated retrospectively in ten patients against approved replans. The QACT is available only on the treatment day, and the experimental plans reused an objective template defined for the future QACT replan and were controlled against that replan's quality score. Thus the dose-domain result is a retrospective feasibility test, not proof that the reported pre-treatment plan workflow would work prospectively. Forecast plans alone lost target coverage when forward-calculated on QACT; reoptimization restored it. This shares its anatomical forecast with [2608.00831](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00831) and [2609.24725](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.24725). The catalog labels only `Forecast`, with plan quality as downstream validation rather than an action-conditioned world-model capability. |
| [Graph World Models for Constrained Epidemic Policy Planning](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.35545), arXiv, submitted 2026-09-28 | **Include.** A graph-factored recurrent state-space model updates regional epidemic beliefs and recursively imagines policy-conditioned joint futures. A constrained planner projects vaccine, capacity, fiscal and NPI allocations into shared budgets. Forecasting and policy planning are evaluated in synthetic and semi-synthetic environments plus a retrospective U.S. state-level panel. Only the simulator tracks permit known counterfactual outcome checks; unexecuted policies in the real panel have model-relative projections, not identified causal effects. The [author code](https://anonymous.4open.science/r/epimind-9706/README.md) was reachable. |
| [SurgGMF: Fully Causal Gaussian Motion Forecasting for Anticipatory Surgical Scene Rendering](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.34733), arXiv, submitted 2026-09-28 | **Include as surgical-state forecasting.** Neural temporal learners predict five future Gaussian position, scale and rotation residual states from ten past states, then render future scene frames without target-frame Gaussian attributes. Twelve EndoNeRF/StereoMIS slices support a chronologically split forecast-and-render evaluation. The horizon is only about 0.125–0.167 seconds, five steps are emitted in one pass, and teacher-derived Gaussian trajectories are not physical tissue ground truth. The paper does not specify whether its per-slice Deform3DGS teacher was fit only to historical frames before each test prediction; its causal-last protocol guarantees no target-frame *attributes at rendering*, not a fully prospective reconstruction pipeline. There is no robot-action-conditioned outcome model or surgical-control test. |
| [Towards Surgical World-Action Modeling: A Preliminary Joint Visual-Trajectory Forecasting for Surgical Motion Planning](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.20284), arXiv, submitted 2026-08-20 | **Include as a backfill.** The model takes five observed frames/tool positions, predicts three future visual states and 2D tool positions per chunk, and feeds its own predictions back for a 15-frame rollout. A SurgWMBench study on clinical prostatectomy suturing videos evaluates visual and trajectory errors across the horizon. It does not condition scene evolution on proposed tool actions or execute a control policy; long-horizon quality degrades substantially. Its use here is `Forecast`, despite “planning” in the title. |

### Recheck of the Five Inclusions

All five satisfy the README's five written criteria on methods-level evidence. EpiMind has the clearest reusable intervention-conditioned world model. The other four are narrower: SurgGMF, the joint surgical visual-trajectory model, and ICU glucose twin are observed-history forecasts; the proton-therapy paper reuses a hybrid anatomical forecast and tests downstream plan adaptation. Their main-list presence follows the README's explicit acceptance of future prediction and procedure-state dynamics, **not** a claim that each supports general counterfactual planning. If the core were narrowed to reusable candidate-action-conditioned transition models, these forecast-only entries would need a separate category.

The follow-up review checked each against all five requirements rather than treating the `world model` or `digital twin` label as evidence:

| Paper | Medical state and implemented transition | Evaluated operational use | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| ICU glycemia | Septic-patient glucose sequence; a PatchTST maps observed glucose history to 15/30 future minute states. | Held-out future glucose forecasting in ten additional patients with retrospective adaptation monitoring. | **Retain, forecast only.** Single-patient pretraining and no insulin response limit generalization and treatment claims. |
| Head-and-neck proton therapy | Planning CT/CTV state; pretrained-registration transport of earlier patients' longitudinal deformations yields treatment-day CT/contour ensemble. | Retrospective QACT comparison and dose-domain plan evaluation in ten cases. | **Retain as a model-family extension, forecast only.** QACT-dependent selection/reoptimization and reuse of the future replan objective template preclude a prospective plan-quality claim. |
| EpiMind | Regional epidemic latent states; graph-factored, intervention-conditioned recurrent transitions. | Recursive forecast and resource-constrained policy planning in synthetic, semi-synthetic, and retrospective real-context tracks. | **Retain.** Real-data alternative-policy effects remain model projections. |
| SurgGMF | Renderable surgical Gaussian geometry; neural learner maps ten past states to five future states. | Future-frame rendering on twelve surgical-video slices using a chronological forecasting split. | **Retain, forecast only.** Teacher-state extraction and short horizon limit end-to-end prospective interpretation. |
| Surgical visual-trajectory model | Surgical visual latent state plus 2D tool position; three-step transition reused for fifteen-step autoregressive prediction. | SurgWMBench future-frame and trajectory evaluation on clinical suturing videos. | **Retain, forecast only.** The paper does not evaluate candidate-action effects or execute planning/control. |

## Recent Candidates Not Promoted

| Candidate | Decision and methods evidence |
|---|---|
| [SurgFlow: 3D Object-Centric Contact Flow for Surgical Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.33237), submitted 2026-09-27 | **Hold outside the stricter core.** A learned generator predicts future object-point paths and tool-contact scores from recent point clouds, and an optimizer maps that desired motion to commands tested on surgical robots. However, the generator is not conditioned on candidate robot actions and does not simulate how alternate commands change subsequent tissue or instrument state. Because the claimed use is control, the README's extra action-effect requirement applies. This is a substantive near-scope forecast-and-policy study, not a validated action-conditioned surgical world model. |
| [A data-driven digital twin of emergency department flow to quantify congestion dynamics and test operational resilience](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11739-026-04540-6), published 2026-09-27 | **Hold for methods access.** The [PubMed record](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42802186/) describes regression-informed stochastic flow simulation and retrospective evaluation, which may fit `Clinical workflow`. Full primary methods could not be inspected through the publisher route, so the abstract is insufficient to verify how patient-flow state and scenario transitions are implemented. |
| [VoxelSage](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.37648), submitted 2026-09-29 | **Outside this new-entry core.** The 3D CT system feeds a resection interface, but the evaluated sequential dynamics and blood-loss proxy belong to a separate frozen 2D synthetic planar simulator. The authors state the simulator omits vessel branching, tissue deformation, perfusion and instrument reachability, and that the CT-to-controller bridge awaits patient-derived validation. The learned component ranks targets rather than learning biological/surgical state transitions. |
| [Augmented Patient Preference Incorporated Reinforcement Learning (APP-RL)](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01044), submitted 2026-10-01 | **Exclude.** Backward-induction decision trees estimate treatment rules and preferences using potential-outcome estimators; the method does not implement or evaluate a patient-state transition model or free-running trajectories. “Dynamic treatment regime” is a decision schedule, not evidence of model dynamics. |
| [Patient-Centered Treatment Planning for Chronic Multimorbidity](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.39911), submitted 2026-09-30 | **Exclude.** An offline hierarchical Q-learning policy consumes observed transition tuples and is evaluated by action agreement and off-policy value estimates. It does not learn a generative patient transition operator or test prospective patient-state rollouts under selected treatments. |
